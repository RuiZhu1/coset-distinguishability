\begin{abstract}\noindent
These notes are the working draft of the four classes of theoretical results in \texttt{ABSTRACT.md}. Every statement carries a status tag
(\st{PROVED}, \st{SKETCH}, \st{NUMERICAL}, \st{CONJECTURE}, \st{PLAN}) and is matched in \S\ref{sec:map}
with a numerical check in the repository: \textbf{theory leads, numerics test the theory}.
Section~0 audits the framework against the axioms of a resource theory (free objects, composition, golden rule, a complete family of monotones, three distinct notions of ``rate'').
Results with a complete written proof: (i) monotonicity under the three free operations; (ii) the representation of erasure, leakage, and burst events in the model, and the boundary of validity;
(iii) the exact upper bound on the marginal exchange rate
$R_{e\to p}\le (3/4-p_0)/(1-e_0)$, which is the sharp bound on the marginal rate of every monotone of the free order (Proposition~\ref{prop:marginal}); (iv) a complete characterization of the preorder of local free operations on the code-capacity Pauli-plus-erasure class;
(v) a finite-sample certificate that can be computed in closed form (code capacity), extended to unflagged bursts and chip-scale events by a single-sum product bound that is uniform in the burst strength;
(vi) an exact counterexample showing that $\err$ is not monotone in the burst strength;
(vii) a Peierls-type finite-sample certificate for the MWPM decoder with erasures;
(viii) a one-sided-derivative form of the rate bound and the exact condition for equality in it (one extra flag never changes the ML decision), which holds for $[[5,1,3]]$ at every rate;
(ix) an explicit finite-$d$ form of the genie (Chernoff) lower bound;
(x) the space-time (circuit-level) form of the burst bound and of the certification theorem, for any linear decoding structure;
(xi) under a marked-Poisson model, the exact gain of pattern methods over counting and the fact that adaptive designs do not beat the best single experiment;
(xii) that the exchange-rate gap of the surface code is code dependence, and a by-hand proof of equality for $[[5,1,3]]$;
(xiii) the tensor-power rates of the uncoded qubit (exact: the one-shot order; approximate: conditional entropy, rate $R_{\rm hash}$);
(xiv) the pure-erasure exponent $\alpha(0,e)=\ln\frac1e-\ln2+O(e)$ of the surface code;
(xv) a Peierls bound with bursts (no floor) and a burst certificate that needs no floor on the burst strength;
(xvi) a Peierls bound for matching on any balanced detector error model, computed for stim's surface-code memory circuit: non-vacuous at circuit-level noise $p=10^{-3}$ for $d\le15$, with a certified suppression factor $\Lambda\ge3.8$ for every $d$, and with bursts at circuit level (no floor, and a certificate without a strength floor).
\emph{Correction} (\S\ref{sec:info}): the union bound sums over all elements of the logical classes, which makes it vacuous for the surface code as $d\to\infty$; the asymptotic statements now rest on Peierls bounds. The remaining items are statements with proof routes or open problems (\S\ref{sec:map}).
\end{abstract}
